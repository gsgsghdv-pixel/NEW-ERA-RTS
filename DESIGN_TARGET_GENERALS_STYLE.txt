NEW ERA RTS design target

Core target: classic base-building RTS structure and pacing inspired by Generals/Zero Hour.
- RTS camera with pan, edge movement and zoom
- resource economy and power
- construction queue and prerequisites
- command center, barracks, war factory, airfield, power and defenses
- infantry, vehicles, artillery and aircraft
- selection, group movement, attack and combat feedback
- fog of war and tactical minimap
- skirmish AI and campaign missions
- 8-player LAN lobby
- lightweight Windows x86_64 performance target
- modern 16:9 presentation and scalable HUD

Implementation goal: reproduce the familiar gameplay structure while adding NEW ERA factions, maps, units, abilities, missions and presentation.
